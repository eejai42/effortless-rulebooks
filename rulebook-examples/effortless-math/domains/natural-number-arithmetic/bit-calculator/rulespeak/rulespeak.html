📘 bit-calculator RuleSpeak

A 4-bit ripple-carry adder as pure rulebook data. 29 wires + 12 gate truth rows. Every result bit is a LOOKUP into a gate truth table keyed on the two driver wires' own computed bits. No arithmetic is stored and no bespoke function exists: ComputedBit is a plain Excel IF over two INDEX/MATCH lookups that recurse down the AWire/BWire self-FKs to the seeded inputs.
